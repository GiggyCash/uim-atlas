# UIM Atlas Privacy Rules

These rules are mandatory for all code, tests, documentation, examples, logs, fixtures, and generated files.

Do not commit personal information.

Do not include real names, personal email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, machine usernames, local home-directory paths, personal RuneScape account names, device names, or other identifying details.

Use neutral placeholders such as `ExamplePlayer`, `TestUim`, `user@example.invalid`, `/home/user/project`, `<username>`, and `<account-name>`.

Do not hard-code developer-specific paths or usernames. Tests and fixtures must use synthetic or anonymized data.

Before committing, review changed files for accidental personal information or machine-specific identifiers and replace them with neutral placeholders.

If a feature appears to require committing personal information, redesign it so that the information is not stored in the repository.
